In your own words. What the data is, where it came from, whether it concerns people, what you may and may not publish, and anything a data steward would need to know. There is no form to fill in because the things that matter are rarely the ones a form anticipates.

What is useful to include
  • Whether it contains personal data, and of what kind.
  • The legal basis: consent, legitimate interest, public task.
  • Any ethics approval, and the body that gave it.
  • Whether anyone else holds rights in it.
  • Any embargo, and until when.
  • Whether it concerns Indigenous peoples or communities.
  • Anything identifying that would not be obvious to a reader — a role held by one person, a place small enough to name someone.
