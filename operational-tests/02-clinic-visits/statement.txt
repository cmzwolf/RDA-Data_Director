Clinic attendance records from a district health post, covering February 2024.
This is sensitive personal health data. Each row is one patient visit and
includes the village, the patient's age and the presenting condition.

Participants consented to the use of their records in research analysis. The
consent does not extend to publication of individual records. Ethics approval
was granted by the national health research committee, reference NHRC-2024-018.
The legal basis is consent. Nothing here may be published in a form that
permits re-identification.
