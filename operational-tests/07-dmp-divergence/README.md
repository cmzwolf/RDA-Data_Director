# 07 · The instruction and the plan disagree

**What this is.** The data management plan commits to Zenodo. The instruction
asks for EUDAT.

**Paste as the instruction:** `Please deposit this in EUDAT rather than Zenodo.`

## What should happen
- The instruction is recorded as coming from you, through the authenticated
  channel.
- A **discrepancy** gate item appears: you asked for EUDAT, the plan promised
  Zenodo.
- It is **flagged, not enforced**. Plans are written years before the data
  exist, and a researcher may have good reason to diverge.
- The note mentions that the funder may expect the plan to be updated.

## What would be a failure
- Silently obeying either one.
- Refusing to proceed.
- Treating the divergence as an error rather than a decision.

## What we are trying to learn
Whether the three sources of authority — your instruction, the plan, a ranking —
behave as intended when two of them conflict.
