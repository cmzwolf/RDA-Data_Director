Seabird counts from two coastal survey sites, recorded during the spring 2024
breeding season. Each row is a site, a date, a species and a count made by a
single observer from a fixed vantage point.

This is observational data about birds. No people are involved, there are no
participants and no consent questions arise. The survey was carried out under
the observatory's standing programme and is intended for open publication under
CC-BY.

The data management plan for this project commits to depositing in Zenodo. I
would like to use EUDAT instead, because our institutional agreement has since
changed.
