# 06 · Material that may engage CARE

**What this is.** Interviews about customary land use with traditional owners.

## What should happen
- A **CARE referral** appears as a gate item, blocking deposit.
- It offers *consulted*, *not applicable* or *exclude* — and **no approve**.
- Recording a consultation requires naming who was consulted.
- The referral points at Local Contexts and GIDA.

## What would be a failure
- No referral raised.
- An *approve* control, which would imply the tool had assessed something.
- Any text suggesting the system has judged CARE compliance. It must not: the
  principles vest authority in the communities concerned, so a system deciding
  on their behalf would violate the principle it claimed to check.

## What we are trying to learn
Whether detection fires on realistic language rather than only on the word
"Indigenous", and whether the referral reads as a referral rather than as a
verdict.
