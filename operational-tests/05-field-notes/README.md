# 05 · A disclosure split across distant passages

**What this is.** Field notes in which no single paragraph identifies anyone.
The district has 412 residents; the principal informant has practised for
nineteen years. Five paragraphs apart, and together they identify one woman.

## What should happen
- Classified **sensitive**.
- Ideally, a stated *combination* citing more than one section — the correlation
  pass joining facts that are individually innocuous.

## What would be a failure
- Classified public or internal. The paragraphs are individually unremarkable,
  so a chunk-local reading finds nothing and says so confidently, which is the
  worst available failure: silence that looks like a clean result.

## What we are trying to learn
Whether the correlation pass earns its place on real prose. Note in passing
whether the model also flags the **administrative officer** and the **officer in
charge** — people who are not research subjects and appear only incidentally.
Earlier testing found it did, which was not anticipated in the design.
